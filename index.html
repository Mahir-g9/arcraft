How to play

  • Aim with the crosshair in the center of the screen by pointing your device.
  • PLACE puts the selected block against the surface you're aiming at.
  • MINE removes the block you're aiming at.
  • Pick a block from the hotbar, or tap ••• for the full inventory.
  • Build a standing rectangle of Obsidian (empty in the middle), select Flint & Steel, aim at the frame and press PLACE to ignite a Nether Portal.
  • Tap the ⟳ button to switch between front and rear cameras (fallback mode only).
